About Alan Joyce & LocalFilm

Founder of localfilm.online • Community-Driven Film & Audio Infrastructure
A Platform Built by Creators, for Creators

Alan Joyce founded localfilm.online and the LocalFilm Network with a clear conviction: in an era of centralized media monopolies and escalating paywalls, independent film and audio storytellers across Ireland's 32 counties need sovereign, high-fidelity creative infrastructure that puts craft and community first.

Why Community & Giving Back Matters

In our fast-paced, hyper-connected yet often isolating modern world, genuine creative communities are rare and vital. Giving back isn't merely an ideal—it is the bedrock of sustainability. LocalFilm was built to bridge Dublin and the regional hubs, from Galway to Wicklow, Cork, Derry, and Donegal, democratizing access to professional studio tools so that no voice is excluded by budget or geography.

Mentorship, shared tools, collaborative audio mixing, and community feedback give emerging directors, sound designers, and independent podcasters the confidence to tell authentic stories that reflect their roots.
