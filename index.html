About Alan Joyce & LocalFilm

Founder Vision & Sovereign Creative Infrastructure • localfilm.online
A Platform Built by Creators, for Creators

Alan Joyce founded localfilm.online and the LocalFilm Network with a clear conviction: in an era of centralized media monopolies and escalating paywalls, independent film and audio storytellers across Ireland's 32 counties need sovereign, high-fidelity creative infrastructure that puts craft and community first.

Why Community & Giving Back Matters

In our fast-paced, hyper-connected yet often isolating modern world, genuine creative communities are rare and vital. Giving back isn't merely an ideal—it is the bedrock of sustainability. LocalFilm was built to bridge Dublin and the regional hubs, from Galway to Wicklow, Cork, Derry, and Donegal, democratizing access to professional studio tools so that no voice is excluded by budget or geography.

Mentorship, shared tools, collaborative audio mixing, and community feedback give emerging directors, sound designers, and independent podcasters the confidence to tell authentic stories that reflect their roots.

v1.8.36 LATEST RELEASE
2026-10-15
Decoupled Transport Ticker Architecture, Stationary Real-Time Recording Waveform & Blank-Timeline First Record Fix
  • Decoupled Transport Ticker Engine: Unlinked recording transport from playback state. The high-resolution RAF timeline ticker (tickDawEngine) now runs autonomously during recording regardless of whether existing clips exist, completely eliminating the first-record stalled playhead bug on empty sessions.
  • Rock-Solid Stationary Waveform Bars: Locked live recording waveform peaks to absolute 20ms timeline buckets with continuous peak interpolation and integer-pixel coordinate rounding (Math.round(b * step)), completely eliminating horizontal vibration, jitter, and squishing during recording and multi-take overdubs.
  • Decoupled Playback Stop: Stopping playback or pausing backing tracks no longer halts or corrupts in-flight audio recording; MediaRecorder and engine ticker remain active until user explicitly presses stop or disarms record.
  • Pre-Emptive Audio Device Discovery: Enumerates input devices on DAW startup and listens for hardware hotplug events (devicechange), resolving stuck "Detecting Mic..." state.
v1.8.35 MILESTONE
2026-10-15
Stationary Real-Time Recording Waveform Engine, Unified AudioContext & Multi-Take Track Synchronization
  • Stationary Live Waveform Rendering: Resolved live waveform squishing and horizontal jittering by locking live waveform bars to stationary timeline time coordinates (barTime = (b * step) / zoom), sampling into fixed 20ms precision buckets so historical peaks remain frozen while new bars append seamlessly at the playhead.
  • Unified Low-Latency AudioContext Pipeline: Eliminated secondary _micMonitorCtx and stream collisions. Mic level VU metering and recording capture now share a single low-latency MediaStreamAudioSourceNode on daw.audioCtx, resolving macOS CoreAudio muting and ensuring live waveform draws on initial record.
  • Multi-Take Track Lane DOM Synchronization: Synchronized auto-created voice-over tracks to ensure track lanes exist in the DOM before mounting recording placeholder clips, guaranteeing immediate waveform canvas rendering on take 2 and consecutive takes.
  • Persistent Hardware Mic Across Takes: Preserves active audio tracks across recording sessions, completely eliminating repeated browser/OS permission prompts and hardware renegotiation audio dropouts.
